Deprivation Map

The English Indices of Deprivation (IoD25) measure relative levels of deprivation in 33,755 small areas or neighbourhoods, called Lower-layer Super Output Areas (LSOAs), in England.

The IoD25 suite of resources comprises 7 standalone indexes combined to form the Index of Multiple Deprivation 2025 (IMD25).

The weightings for the multi-score measure of deprivation are:

    Income - 22.5%

    Employment - 22.5%

  • Education - 13.5%
  • Health - 13.5%
  • Crime - 9.3%
  • Barriers to Housing & Services - 9.3%
  • Living Environment - 9.3%

The map also records the TUNDRA scores at the LSOA level (proportion of 16-year-olds in higher education by the age of 18/19).

Data Source: UK Government: English Indices of Deprivation and Office for Students
